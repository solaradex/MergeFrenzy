# Zero-cost monetization

Google states AdSense participation is free, but site approval and policy compliance are required.

When approved, add only the exact AdSense code/units supplied to your account. Add `ads.txt` only when your ad provider gives you the exact publisher record. Update the privacy notice for actual advertising/consent behavior.

Rewarded game benefits must be tied to a real provider's completed rewarded-ad callback, not a client-side timer.

No paid backend is required.
